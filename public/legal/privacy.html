Privacy Policy

Effective 31 Jul 2026 · Version 1.0

Privacy Terms

This Privacy Policy explains how FastRapid (“we”, “us”) collects, uses, and protects personal data when you use our websites, mobile apps, and related services as a customer, merchant, or rider in Nigeria.

1. Data we collect

  • Account details: name, email, phone number, and authentication preferences.
  • Order and delivery data: addresses, cart contents, payment references, and delivery status.
  • Merchant and rider verification documents submitted for onboarding review.
  • Device and usage data: app events used to improve discovery, reliability, and fraud prevention.
  • Location data when you enable location features for delivery or rider tracking.

2. How we use data

  • To create and secure accounts, process orders, and settle wallet payments.
  • To assign and track deliveries, and to notify you about order status.
  • To review merchant and rider applications and keep the marketplace safe.
  • To improve product performance, measure conversion funnels, and prevent abuse.
  • To meet legal, regulatory, and dispute-resolution obligations.

3. Sharing

We share data with service providers required to operate FastRapid (for example payment processors, messaging providers, maps, and cloud hosting). We do not sell personal data. Merchants and riders only receive information needed to fulfil their orders or deliveries.

4. Retention

We retain account and transaction records for as long as needed to provide the service, meet financial and legal obligations, and resolve disputes. You may request deletion of your account subject to outstanding obligations and legal retention requirements.

5. Your rights

Subject to applicable Nigerian data-protection law, you may request access to your data, correction of inaccurate information, restriction of processing, or deletion of your account. Use in-app Privacy & Security request options or email support@fastrapid.com.

6. Security

We use industry-standard controls including encrypted transport, authenticated APIs, optional two-factor authentication, and access-limited admin tooling. No method of transmission is perfectly secure; please protect your devices and credentials.

7. Children

FastRapid is intended for users who can form a binding contract under Nigerian law. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children for account creation.

8. Contact

Privacy questions: support@fastrapid.com. We will update this page when our practices change and revise the effective date above.
