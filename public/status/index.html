Key takeaways

  1. Login & security is launch-ready except strict phone verification (deferred until production SMS is validated). Customers can sign in from browsers even with biometric enabled on mobile.
  2. Customer ordering pricing integrity, cancel/refund, and live rider tracking are in place; simulated crypto checkout is off.
  3. Payments & wallet money-path integrity is hardened; live bill purchases still need VTpass keys configured.
  4. Merchants can onboard, manage menu, pause the store, set hours, and take orders; Paystack withdraw smoke still needs staging funding.
  5. Riders can deliver, manage payout banks, and cash out; wallet history shows credits and withdrawals.
  6. Admin ops can use live ledger, withdrawals, disputes, support, settings, analytics, and the rider map; dispute auto-refund stays later.
  7. Android packaging is ahead of iOS; on-device push QA and TestFlight remain the next mobile milestones.
